# Submissions Overview
The Submissions section is under **Talent Marketing** in the left navigation. It lists all candidates who have been submitted to clients for open positions and provides visibility into submission status and hiring progress.

## Navigating to Submissions
Go to **Talent Marketing → ATS - Submissions** in the left sidebar. You can also access it directly at `/talent-marketing/ats/submissions`.

## Filtering and Views
Use the tabs at the top of the page to filter submissions:

- **All** — view all submissions
- **Active** — view submissions currently in progress
- **Inactive** — view submissions that are no longer active

Additional options are available in the toolbar:
- **Filter** (funnel icon) —  Filter applications based on  application status and contact status
- **Search** (magnifying glass) — Find submissions by candidate name, job title, submission date, or submitted by
- **Export** — Download submission data
- **Fixed Columns** — Switch between available views
- **Row Height** — Adjust the height of rows by minimizing or maximizing row size for better visibility and screen usage

## Submission Information

The submissions grid displays the following details:
- **Candidate Name** — Name of the submitted candidate
- **Job Title** — Position for which the candidate was submitted
- **Submission Date** — Date the candidate was submitted to the client
- **Submitted By** — Recruiter or user who submitted the candidate
- **Status** — Current status of the submission
- **Options** — Additional actions available for the submission

## Submission Statuses

Common submission statuses include:
- **Interview** — Candidate is progressing through the interview process
- **Joined** — Candidate has accepted the offer and joined
- **Placed** — Candidate has been successfully placed
- **Client Rejected** — Candidate was rejected by the client
- **Inactive** — Submission is no longer active

## Managing Submissions

From the submissions page, users can:
- Monitor candidate submission progress
- Track recruiter activity
- Review submission history
- Update submission records
- Access submission-specific actions through the Options menu
